# The five reply shapes

Every field listed is required. If you cannot fill one, say so on the line rather than dropping it.

## 1 · Xem trước · Preview  (page + 3 lines in chat)
- **Environment strip**: product, staging or production, which hotel's data, size and credits.
- **Their words**, quoted exactly, in their language.
- **Three readings** of the request, each a small picture, one marked "đề xuất / recommended".
- **Before / after** of the real screen. Before is a screenshot or labelled a sketch.
- **Numbered changes** — each one a thing a person would notice.
- **Đã có sẵn / Already exists** — what is being extended rather than created, and any competing data source.
- **Operator buttons**: `✓ Duyệt` · `✎ Sửa #n` · `Tách nhỏ`.
- Never a third frame here. Before and after only.
- **If a picture was attached**, it is the specification: the "after" frame reproduces it, colours included. Say in one line what you took from it.
- **If they marked it urgent** (`gấp`, `urgent`, `critical`): say which of the two urgent slots it takes and what it pushes behind it. Two urgent items at a time, per product, is the cap — with no cap, everything becomes urgent and the word stops working.

## 2 · Bằng chứng · Proof  (page + 3 lines in chat)
- Strip: ticket id, environment, build time, hotels it is on, hotels it is not on.
- **Three frames**: approved picture · what is live now (screenshot, timestamped) · differences.
- **Checklist** of the numbered changes, each passed, fixed-then-passed, or failed with what you saw.
- **Xem ở đâu / Where it lives**: the screen in staff words, the link, hotels on and off, a 30-second check.
- Verdict words from `honesty.md`.

## 3 · Cần bạn · Needs-me  (page when more than one item)
- Numbered rows, three types: **Duyệt** approve · **Chọn** pick · **Gỡ kẹt** unblock.
- Each row: what it changes, what it costs, who raised it, how long it has waited, **the default and when it applies**.
- Ranked by what is blocking the most other work.

## 4 · Bản tin · Brief  (chat only)
- `Đã xong 24h qua` with a block bar and a count, each line in what-a-person-sees language.
- `Vẫn kẹt` with owner and age in days; three days or more is called out.
- One closing line: the single thing that needs this person, pointing at `/needs-me`.
- Under about twenty lines. No page.

## 5 · Kẹt · Stall alert  (chat only)
Raised whenever `/brief`, `/needs-me` or `/track` meets something that has not moved for 12 hours — the kit cannot push a message on its own, so it surfaces this the moment anyone runs a command. Say how long it has been stuck.
- What it is waiting on, in one sentence, with both sides of the disagreement if there is one.
- **One** owner, **one** action, a time estimate.
- The default, with its time.
- What breaks if nobody acts.
- Technical detail only behind a `▸ cho kỹ thuật` fold.

## 6 · Đã lên · Release note  (chat only, produced by `/proof` when the verdict is live)
- What staff now see, in their words.
- Where: screen, link.
- Hotels on · hotels not on.
- A 30-second check.

## 7 · Số liệu · Numbers  (chat, or a chart when it has a shape)
- The figure, large and plain.
- **How it is calculated**, in one line, in words — not a formula.
- **Where it came from**: which source, read at what time.
- **What is in and what is out**: which hotels, which dates, which rows were dropped and why. A total that silently omits rows is the single fastest way to lose trust, and it has already happened here.
- If two sources disagree, show both and say which you would trust — never pick one silently.
- One or two figures stay in chat. A trend or a breakdown becomes a chart, and every label names a real value.
